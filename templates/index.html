About Varsell

Built to give every entrepreneur a real online store—without the heavy price tag.

Thousands of business owners across Ghana and Africa struggle to pay expensive developer fees just to get an e-commerce website—leaving them stuck posting dozens of photos on their WhatsApp status every single day.

Varsell solves this by hosting thousands of fast, mobile-first cloud stores on one unified platform. Every merchant who signs up gets a private Secret Key to manage their catalog, a clean web-searchable store link, built-in Mobile Money (MoMo) checkout, and offline Progressive Web App (PWA) technology so customers can shop anytime, anywhere.

🚀 Zero Coding or Fees

Sign up in 60 seconds, unlock your store with your custom key, and start posting products immediately.

🌐 Searchable Short Links

Every store gets an indexed, easy-to-remember web link and automatic sitemap registration for search engines.

📲 Offline-First PWA

Customers can install your store on their phone and browse your catalog even with 0MB mobile data.

📲 Install Varsell on Your Phone

Installing Varsell adds it to your home screen and lets you open stores and view products **even when your data is off**:

🤖 On Android / Chrome: Tap the 3 dots (⋮) in the top right of Chrome and select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".

🍏 On iPhone (Safari): Tap the Share icon (⬆️) at the bottom and tap "Add to Home Screen".
