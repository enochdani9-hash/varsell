🌍 Seller & Buyer Help Guide

1. How do I change my Store Theme & Profile Picture?

Inside your dashboard, click "🎨 Store Look & Avatar" (or tap your profile icon in the top-left corner) to upload a new profile picture, add a cover banner, and choose from 6 custom themes!

2. Can I change my Store Name and Web Link later?

Yes! Click "✏️ Change Store Name" on your dashboard Link Card. Your store title and URL will update immediately, and your old link will automatically redirect to your new name!

3. Can I use Varsell outside Ghana?

Yes! Varsell supports international WhatsApp dial codes, 9+ global currencies (USD, GBP, EUR, GHS, NGN, KES, ZAR, CAD, XOF), and flexible payment methods (MoMo, Bank Transfer, CashApp, Zelle, PayPal, M-Pesa).

4. How does Offline Browsing work?

Tap "📲 Install App" at the top of the page. Once installed, any store you visit is cached on your phone so you can browse products even with 0MB mobile data.

📲 Install Varsell on Your Device

Installing Varsell adds it to your home screen and lets you open stores and view products even when your internet connection is off:

🤖 On Android / Chrome: Tap the 3 dots (⋮) in the top right of Chrome and select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".

🍏 On iPhone (Safari): Tap the Share icon (⬆️) at the bottom and tap "Add to Home Screen".
