🌍 Seller & Buyer Help Guide

1. What is a 👑 Verified Pro Store?

Verified Pro stores receive an official gold verification badge and priority #1 placement at the top of the Varsell Global Store Directory so more buyers discover their products.

2. How do I change my Store Theme, Background & Profile Picture?

Inside your dashboard, tap "🎨 Look & Background" (or tap your profile icon in the top-left corner) to upload a new profile picture, set a full-screen background wallpaper, and choose from 6 custom themes!

3. Can I change my Store Name and Web Link later?

Yes! Tap "✏️ Change Name" on your dashboard Link Card. Your store title and URL will update immediately, and your old link will automatically redirect to your new name!

📲 Install Varsell on Your Device

🤖 On Android / Chrome: Tap the 3 dots (⋮) in the top right of Chrome and select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".

🍏 On iPhone (Safari): Tap the Share icon (⬆️) at the bottom and tap "Add to Home Screen".
