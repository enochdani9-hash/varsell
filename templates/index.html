❓ Seller & Buyer Help Guide

1. Can I change my Store Name and Link later?

Yes! Inside your dashboard, click "✏️ Customize Name" on your Store Link card. You can change your store name and URL anytime—and your old link will automatically redirect to your new name!

2. What if I want to change my Secret Key?

Click "✏️ Customize Key" inside your dashboard to set any secret password you prefer.

3. How does Offline Browsing work?

Tap "📲 Install App" at the top of the page. Once installed, any store you open is saved on your phone so you can browse products even with 0MB data.

📲 Install Varsell on Your Phone

Installing Varsell adds it to your home screen and lets you open stores and view products even when your mobile data is off:

🤖 On Android / Chrome: Tap the 3 dots (⋮) in the top right of Chrome and select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".

🍏 On iPhone (Safari): Tap the Share icon (⬆️) at the bottom and tap "Add to Home Screen".
